## Supported network model

Parly uses Tempo as the private payment and settlement hub.

Supported connected networks include Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, and BSC where active routes are available.

## Cross-chain deposits

Cross-chain deposits let a payer start from a supported source network and route value into private Parly balance on Tempo.

The App shows the available route, asset, amount, fees, refund path, and status before a user commits.

## Cross-chain payouts

Cross-chain payouts let private Parly balance settle to supported destination routes where the payout route is active.

Availability depends on route configuration, relayer availability, policy checks, and current network conditions.

Before you authorize a payout, Parly shows the exact destination amount and separates the protocol, source-execution, route, and relayer fees that apply. The protocol and source-execution fees are charged when funds leave your private balance and are not refundable.

Each payout is tracked through distinct stages:

1. the route is authorized
2. the Tempo withdrawal confirms
3. funds reach the payout escrow
4. Relay processes the destination delivery
5. delivery or refund is confirmed from objective chain evidence

An accepted source transaction is not the same as a completed destination payment. Parly shows a payout as delivered only after the destination transfer is proven.

If Relay returns funds, network refund costs or an origin swap may reduce the returned amount or change the returned stablecoin. A refund becomes claimable only after the return is proven. Connect the wallet bound to the payout to claim it.

## Route safety

If a route is unavailable, Parly should fail closed instead of asking the user to send funds manually.

Do not send funds to an address, contract, or route that was not generated by the current Parly flow.

Keep the support reference shown for a delayed payout. It helps support locate the route, but it cannot authorize a status lookup, receipt, or refund.

## Relayers

Relayers help process supported private execution work. A relayer may support ingress duties, payout duties, or both depending on its configuration and the active route.

Relayers do not receive your private keys or full private ledger.
