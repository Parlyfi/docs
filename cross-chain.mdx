## Supported network model

Parly uses Tempo as the private payment and settlement hub.

Supported connected networks include Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, and BSC where active routes are available.

## Cross-chain deposits

Cross-chain deposits let a payer start from a supported source network and route value into private Parly balance on Tempo.

The App shows the available route, asset, amount, fees, refund path, and status before a user commits.

## Cross-chain payouts

Cross-chain payouts let private Parly balance settle to supported destination routes where the payout route is active.

Availability depends on route configuration, relayer availability, policy checks, and current network conditions.

## Route safety

If a route is unavailable, Parly should fail closed instead of asking the user to send funds manually.

Do not send funds to an address, contract, or route that was not generated by the current Parly flow.

## Relayers

Relayers help process supported private execution work. A relayer may support ingress duties, payout duties, or both depending on its configuration and the active route.

Relayers do not receive your private keys or full private ledger.
