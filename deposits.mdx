## Deposit methods

Parly supports two user-facing deposit methods where routes are available.

## Deposit from wallet

Deposit from wallet is the connected-wallet path.

Use it when you want to pay directly from the wallet connected to the App or a public payment link.

The flow may ask you to:

1. choose network and asset
2. enter the amount
3. review route and fees
4. approve the asset if approval is required
5. sign or submit the transaction
6. wait for settlement and private-balance recovery

## One-time deposit address

A one-time deposit address is generated for a specific payment instruction.

Use it when you want to send from a supported wallet, exchange, or app that cannot connect directly to Parly.

The flow may ask you to:

1. choose network, asset, and amount
2. provide a refund wallet you control
3. confirm the source acknowledgement
4. generate the one-time address
5. send exactly the displayed asset and amount before expiry
6. wait for payment detection and settlement

Do not reuse an expired address or send a different asset than the one displayed.

## Supported assets

Supported assets depend on the selected route.

Tempo routes may show Tempo-native labels such as USDC.e and USDT0. Other supported networks may show USDC and USDT where those routes are available.

## Status states

Deposit flows can show:

- awaiting payment
- pending
- completed
- failed
- expired
- refund available

If a state is unclear, use the receipt, status page, or support path shown by the App. Do not send a second payment unless the App gives a fresh instruction.
