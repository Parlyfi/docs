## What an invoice link is

A Privacy Invoice Link is a public payment page for a specific requested payment.

Use it when you want to request a defined amount, asset, expiry, and recipient context while keeping your private Parly balance and unrelated payment history private.

## Create an invoice

1. Open the App and authenticate.
2. Open **Privacy Links**.
3. Choose **Invoice Links**.
4. Enter the invoice label, amount, asset, expiry, and optional public details.
5. Choose whether to show public profile details such as Parly username, X profile, or website.
6. Preview the invoice.
7. Publish the invoice link.

The invoice URL should not be treated as payable until it is published.

## What payers see

Payers see:

- invoice title and amount
- selected asset and network options
- expiry state
- deposit from wallet where available
- one-time deposit address where available
- receipt and verification options after payment completion

## Settlement verification

After payment completes, an invoice receipt or verification link can be used to verify settlement.

Invoice verification confirms settlement only. It does not confirm goods or services delivery, identity, or private business terms.

## Editing invoices

Before publication, you can edit the invoice draft.

After publication, editing should preserve settled receipt history. If a material payment detail changes, create a new invoice link so payers do not rely on outdated payment instructions.

## Expired and failed invoices

If an invoice expires or a payment fails, follow the status shown on the invoice page. Do not reuse old instructions or send funds after the app marks the route unavailable.
