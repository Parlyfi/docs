## What the ledger is

The App ledger helps you review Parly activity and prepare scoped receipts.

It is designed to show the payment records you need without exposing unrelated private balance history.

## What receipts are for

Receipts are selective disclosure records.

A receipt may show:

- payment or invoice status
- amount and asset
- route or network
- settlement date
- public link context where intentionally disclosed
- verification link or verification instructions

Receipts do not prove goods or services delivery, identity, or private business terms.

## Payer history

Where supported, a payer can recover redacted payment history by connecting the refund wallet used for a one-time deposit.

This is designed for practical reconciliation without exposing payer wallets, private balances, or the recipient's private ledger.

## Exporting records

The App may support:

- receipt PDF download
- visible ledger CSV export
- scoped payout verification handoff
- invoice settlement verification handoff

Only share receipts and verification links with people you intend to disclose that payment to.
