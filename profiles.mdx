## What a profile link is

A Parly Profile Link is a public page such as `parly.fi/yourname` where someone can send you a supported stablecoin payment.

The payer sees your public profile details and chooses an available payment method. The payment settles into your private Parly balance when the route completes.

## Create a profile link

1. Open the App and connect your wallet.
2. Authenticate your wallet so Parly can load your private state.
3. Open **Privacy Links**.
4. Reserve a Parly username.
5. Add your display name, profile image, optional cover image, bio, website, and X profile if you want them public.
6. Choose the supported assets and networks you want to accept.
7. Publish the profile link.

Your profile URL should not be treated as live until the profile is published.

## What payers see

Payers see one payment page with:

- your public profile details
- amount entry
- supported asset selection
- supported network selection
- deposit from wallet where available
- one-time deposit address where available
- receipt and verification options after payment completion

## Optional payer details

Where supported, a payer may add a display name or reference. This helps the profile owner reconcile payments.

Do not put sensitive personal information into payer reference fields.

## Editing and renaming

You can edit public profile details from the Privacy Links tab.

If you change a username, old public links may stop resolving or may show a restricted/unavailable state. Receipts and payment records should continue to refer to the payment they were created for.

## Notifications

Where configured, you can connect notification channels such as Telegram to receive payment alerts.

Notifications should never require you to expose private keys or seed phrases.
