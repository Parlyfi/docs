## What invoice verification proves

Invoice verification confirms settlement only for a specific invoice payment through Parly.

It does not confirm goods or services delivery, identity, or private business terms.

## What you need

Use the verification token or verification link from the receipt.

If you have a direct verification link, opening it should load the verification result automatically.

## What may be shown

Depending on what the receipt intentionally discloses, Verify may show:

- invoice label
- amount and asset
- network
- settlement date
- receipt identifier
- public link
- Parly username
- X profile

Verify does not show the recipient's private Parly balance or unrelated payments.

## Typical flow

1. Open the invoice receipt or verification link.
2. Review the settlement result.
3. Check that the invoice details match the payment you expected.
4. Keep the receipt for reconciliation.
